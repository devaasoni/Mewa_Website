FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

What is The Mewa?

The Mewa is a SEBI-registered stock recommendation platform that provides expert-curated buy/sell calls with real-time tracking, revision history, and detailed research reports to help you make informed trading decisions.

How do I receive recommendations?

You'll receive instant notifications via our mobile app and web platform whenever a new recommendation is published. Each alert includes the stock name, action (buy/sell), entry price range, target, and stop loss.

What is your success rate?

Our historical success rate is approximately 87% over the last 6 months. You can view our complete track record in the Past Performance section, which is updated in real-time with every trade outcome.

Is The Mewa SEBI registered?

Yes, The Mewa is fully SEBI registered as a Research Analyst. We comply with all regulatory requirements and maintain complete transparency in our operations and recommendations.

Can I track revision history?

Every recommendation card shows complete revision history. You can see when targets or stop losses were updated, the previous values, and timestamps for each change - ensuring full transparency.
